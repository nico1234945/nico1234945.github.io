Award Eligibility

My poetry and short fiction is eligible for awards including the Nebula, Hugo, and Rhysling. My top poem and short story for awards consideration are included below; you can check out all my works published in 2026 on my Awards Eligibility page (coming soon!).

The Mermaid Speaks of Social Justice from the Bathtub (poetry, 41 lines, fantasy/weird). Published in Strange Horizons (May 2026).

Change requires examination of the initial errors,
   of the circumstances from   then
   that led to the injustice   of now.

My Ma's Hatred Grows Cavities Every Summer (short story, 1000 words, horror). Published in The Rumpus (September 2026).

She would get a terrible, terrible glimpse of the pain she’d forced onto me every day.
And after that?
I could not say that I cared.
